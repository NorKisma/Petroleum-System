Welcome to Rays POS & Accounting. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard the data of our tenants and their customers.

We implement professional security measures to maintain the safety of your personal and business information. All sensitive data is encrypted and stored securely.
