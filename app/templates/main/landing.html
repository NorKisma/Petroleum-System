System Capabilities

Core Features

Pump Management

Monitor real-time sales across all nozzles. Map pumps to tanks seamlessly and track opening/closing meter readings per shift.

Tank & Dip Readings

Maintain precise fuel levels. Log physical dip readings and automatically calculate variations (gain/loss) against theoretical stock.

Fleet Sales (Credit)

Manage corporate clients with ease. Track vehicle plates, driver details, and issue consolidated invoices for post-paid fuel sales.

Shift Reconciliation

Automated shift closing. Compare pump sales, credit sales, expense deductions, and actual cash collected by the attendant.

Analytics & Reports

Generate comprehensive daily, weekly, and monthly reports. Visualize your revenue, profit margins, and inventory trends.

Financial Accounting

Built-in double entry accounting. Manage expenses, vendor payments, chart of accounts, and monitor overall business financial health.
